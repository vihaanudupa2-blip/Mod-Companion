Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data SentinelBot (U-bot#9111) collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using U-bot, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

U-bot collects only the minimum data required to operate its features:

  • Guild (Server) Settings — configuration such as welcome channel, log channel, moderation thresholds, and reaction role mappings. Stored per-server.
  • Moderation Records — warnings, kicks, bans, and mutes issued via bot commands, including the target user ID, moderator user ID, reason, and timestamp.
  • User IDs and Message IDs — used transiently for features like reaction roles and auto-moderation. No message content is persistently stored.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Message content (beyond what is needed for real-time auto-moderation evaluation)
  • Voice data or attachments
  • Personal information such as email addresses, real names, or payment details
  • Data from servers U-bot is not a member of

4. How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Deliver the bot's core features (moderation, welcome messages, reaction roles, etc.)
  • Allow server administrators to configure per-server settings
  • Maintain moderation audit logs accessible to server administrators

We do not sell, share, or use collected data for advertising or profiling purposes.

5. Data Retention

Server configuration and moderation records are retained as long as U-bot remains in your server. When U-bot is removed from a server, you may request deletion of all associated data by contacting us through the support server. We will process such requests within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data, including using encrypted database connections and restricting access to production systems. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

U-bot operates within Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

U-bot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of U-bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please reach out through the official SentinelBot support server.
